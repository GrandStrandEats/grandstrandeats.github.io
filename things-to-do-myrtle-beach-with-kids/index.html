What is there for kids to do in Myrtle Beach?

Kids love Ripley's Aquarium, WonderWorks, Alligator Adventure, mini golf, go-karts and the Pirates Voyage dinner show. Many of them are clustered around Broadway at the Beach and Barefoot Landing.

For outdoor time, the state parks have nature trails, marsh boardwalks and quieter beaches, and the piers are great for a first fishing trip.

What can kids do in Myrtle Beach on a rainy day?

Ripley's Aquarium, WonderWorks and the Hollywood Wax Museum are all indoors at Broadway at the Beach. The dinner shows are indoors too.

What is cheap to do with kids in Myrtle Beach?

The beach, the Boardwalk, the L.W. Paul Living History Farm and Grand Park cost nothing. A state park day costs a few dollars per person, and a Pelicans baseball game is one of the cheapest nights out in summer.

Local tips

  • Buy aquarium and show tickets online ahead of busy weeks.
  • Go to indoor attractions early on rainy days. Everyone else has the same idea by noon.
  • Watch for alligators (from a distance!) along the Huntington Beach State Park causeway.
  • Many go-kart and mini golf spots cut back hours in the off-season.

Common questions

What is good to do in Myrtle Beach with toddlers?

Ripley's Aquarium, the beach at Myrtle Beach State Park, and the farm animals at the free L.W. Paul Living History Farm are all good picks for little ones.

Is Myrtle Beach good for teenagers?

Yes. Teens tend to like WonderWorks, the go-kart tracks, the SkyWheel and the Boardwalk at night.
