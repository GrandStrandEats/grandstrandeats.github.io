What can you do in Myrtle Beach when it rains?

Go to Ripley's Aquarium, WonderWorks or the Hollywood Wax Museum, catch a show, or visit a museum. Those three indoor attractions are all at Broadway at the Beach.

Shows at the Carolina Opry, Alabama Theatre, Legends in Concert, Pirates Voyage and Medieval Times are a good way to wait out a storm.

Do summer storms in Myrtle Beach last all day?

Often not. Summer storms on the Grand Strand tend to pop up in the afternoon and pass within an hour or two. Plan an indoor stop for mid-afternoon and you may get the beach back by evening.

Local tips

  • Indoor attractions fill up fast on rainy days. Go early or buy tickets online.
  • Lightning closes the beach and piers. Wait 30 minutes after the last thunder.
  • The Horry County Museum in Conway is free and indoors on weekdays.

Common questions

What is the best indoor attraction in Myrtle Beach?

Ripley's Aquarium is one of the most popular, with an underwater tunnel full of sharks and sea turtles.

Are Myrtle Beach shows open year-round?

Most of the big theaters run shows much of the year, with Christmas shows from early November. Check each theater's schedule.
