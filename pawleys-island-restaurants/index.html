Pawleys Island and Litchfield restaurants

Pawleys Island and Litchfield Beach are the quiet south end of the Grand Strand. Pawleys likes to call itself "arrogantly shabby," and the restaurants fit: relaxed, local and unfussy, with some special-occasion spots mixed in. Local tips

  • Most of the restaurants are along Highway 17, not on the island itself.
  • It's about 30 minutes south of Myrtle Beach, and an easy day trip from Murrells Inlet.
  • Historic Georgetown is about 15 minutes farther south if you want to make a day of it.
Hours, prices and vibes change, and some listings are still being checked. Call ahead or check the restaurant's website before you go. Know a local spot we're missing? Tell us on our Facebook page.
