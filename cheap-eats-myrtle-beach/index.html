Where can I eat cheap in Myrtle Beach?

For cheap food in Myrtle Beach, go for barbecue, tacos, soul food or Jamaican. Little Pigs BBQ, Fullbelly BBQ and Simply Southern Smokehouse do barbecue, Tu Taco does tacos, Big Mike's Soul Food does soul food, and Reggae Island Grill does Jamaican.

Breakfast is the cheapest meal out. Donald's Pancake House, Pan American Pancake & Omelette House and Blueberry's Grill all show one dollar sign.

Where can I eat cheap in North Myrtle Beach and Surfside Beach?

In North Myrtle Beach, try Big D's BBQ Trough, Taco Mundo, Sunday Best Jamaican Cuisine, Hoskin's Restaurant or Kitchen Table. Hot Stacks Pancake House is the budget breakfast pick.

In Surfside Beach, try Hamburger Joe's, River City Cafe, Fat Jack's Wings & Things or Pizza Hyena. For breakfast, Surf Diner, Wooden Spoon, Golden Egg Pancake House and Johnny D's Waffles are all one dollar sign.

Murrells Inlet and Garden City. Sam's Corner does hot dogs, River City Cafe does burgers, and the Pier Snack Bar in Garden City sits right on the pier.

Is Conway cheaper than Myrtle Beach for eating out?

Often, yes. Conway's restaurants cater mostly to locals and college students, and many show one dollar sign on our map. Try Cheesesteak U, Kosta Bowls, Bonfire Taqueria, El Patio, CW's Wings and Ribs, Chantis or Trestle Restaurant. See our Conway guide.

How can I save money eating out at the beach?

Eat your big meal at lunch, share plates, and skip the oceanfront tourist strip for dinner. Lunch menus are usually cheaper than dinner, and spots a few blocks off the ocean often cost less than the ones on it.

Breakfast out and dinner in the condo is a popular budget split. Pancake houses are cheap, filling and great with kids.

Local tips

  • Barbecue, tacos and pizza are the cheapest ways to feed a group.
  • Eat your main meal at lunch. Many sit-down spots charge less at midday.
  • Drinks add up fast. Water with dinner saves a lot over a week.
  • Places a few blocks off the ocean often cost less than oceanfront ones.
  • Look for local daily specials on a restaurant's Facebook page before you go.
