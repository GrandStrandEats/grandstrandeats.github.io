Where can I get local pizza near me on the Grand Strand?

Every town on the Grand Strand has at least one local pizza place, so start with the one closest to where you're staying. Summer traffic on Kings Highway and Highway 17 can turn a short pizza run into a long one.

Myrtle Beach. Scottos Pizzeria, Marco Polo Pizzeria, Piana's Pizza, River Oaks Pizzeria, Pizza de Roma, Amici Pizza and Genova's Pizza & Pasta are all local pizza shops. Hot Tomato and Ducati's Pizza Trattoria are sit-down Italian spots that also do pizza.

North Myrtle Beach. Daniello's Pizzeria & Pub, North Beach Pizza Co., Georgio's Famous Pizza, The Main Slice, Basil's Pizza and Crave Italian Oven & Bar. Papa's Pizza is just up the road in Little River.

Surfside Beach and Garden City. Pizza Hyena, Anthony's Pan Pizza and Casa Calamari are in Surfside Beach. Francesco's Pizza Kitchen, Aromas, Milardo's Pizzeria and Franco's Pizza are in Garden City.

Murrells Inlet and Pawleys Island. Danny's Pizza and a second Francesco's Pizza Kitchen are in Murrells Inlet. DeRoMa's Pizzeria, Rosie's Pizzeria and South End Bistro are in Pawleys Island.

The Market Common and Conway. Mama Mia Pizzeria and Ultimate California Pizza are at the Market Common. Anto's Pizza Romana and Chantis are in Conway.

Which pizza place is best for my group?

For kids and big groups, pick a casual pizzeria and order whole pies. For a nicer night out, pick a sit-down Italian spot that also makes pizza.

With kids. Almost every pizzeria on this list is marked family-friendly on our map, and most show one dollar sign, which makes pizza one of the cheapest ways to feed a group at the beach.

With a dog. Pizza Hyena in Surfside Beach is marked dog-friendly on our map. Call ahead to ask about patio seating.

A nicer dinner. Crave Italian Oven & Bar, Hot Tomato, Ducati's Pizza Trattoria and Casa Calamari are sit-down restaurants where part of the table can order pasta. Bovine's Wood Fired Specialties on the Murrells Inlet MarshWalk is the upscale pick, with a water view.

When is the best time to order pizza in Myrtle Beach?

Order before 5:30 PM or after 8 PM in summer to skip the longest waits. Friday and Saturday evenings are the busiest, and rainy days are busy all day because everyone has the same idea.

Many local shops close earlier in the off-season, and some close one day a week. Use the open-now filter on the map or call before you drive over.

Local tips

  • Call your order in before you leave the beach. Picking it up on the way back to the condo beats waiting in a full lobby.
  • Want delivery? Ask if the shop delivers to your rental or resort before you count on it.
  • Whole pies usually cost less per person than slices or individual plates, which helps with a big group.
  • Check hours in the off-season. Smaller shops sometimes close early or take a day off each week.
  • Staying a full week? Try a different local shop each time instead of the national chains. That's half the fun.

Common questions

Where can I get good local pizza in Myrtle Beach?

Local pizza shops in Myrtle Beach include Scottos Pizzeria, Marco Polo Pizzeria, Piana's Pizza, River Oaks Pizzeria, Pizza de Roma and Amici Pizza. For a sit-down meal with pizza on the menu, try Hot Tomato or Ducati's Pizza Trattoria.

Is there local pizza in North Myrtle Beach?

Yes. Daniello's Pizzeria & Pub, North Beach Pizza Co., Georgio's Famous Pizza, The Main Slice, Basil's Pizza and Crave Italian Oven & Bar are all in North Myrtle Beach.

Where can I get pizza in Surfside Beach or Garden City?

In Surfside Beach, try Pizza Hyena, Anthony's Pan Pizza or Casa Calamari. In Garden City, try Francesco's Pizza Kitchen, Aromas, Milardo's Pizzeria or Franco's Pizza.
