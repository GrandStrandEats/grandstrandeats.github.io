Where can I find Mexican food near me on the Grand Strand?

There's a Mexican restaurant in nearly every town here, so start with the one closest to you.

Myrtle Beach. Tu Taco, Taqueria Guanajuato, Mexico Lindo, Margaritas Mexican Restaurant and El Cerro Mexican Bar & Grill. Señor Frogs is a Mexican party bar with live music.

North Myrtle Beach and Little River. Craft Taco, Roca Roja Cantina, La Promesa Taqueria Y Pupuseria and El Cerro Mexican Bar & Grill are in North Myrtle Beach. Casa Villa Mexican Restaurant is in Little River.

Conway. Bonfire Taqueria, El Patio, Margaritas Mexican Restaurant and El Cerro Mexican Bar and Grill.

South end. Los Cabos is in Surfside Beach, El Cerro Grande is in Garden City, LUNA Mexican Bar & Grill is in Murrells Inlet, and Taqueria Las Comadres Express and El Cerro are in Socastee. There's also an El Cerro Grande in Carolina Forest and one in Georgetown.

Which Mexican restaurant should I pick?

Pick a taqueria for a quick, cheap meal, a sit-down restaurant for a group, and LUNA for a date night.

Quick tacos. Tu Taco, Taqueria Guanajuato, La Promesa Taqueria Y Pupuseria, Bonfire Taqueria and Taqueria Las Comadres Express are the taco-shop style places on our list.

With kids or a group. Margaritas Mexican Restaurant and Mexico Lindo in Myrtle Beach are marked family-friendly on our map.

Date night. LUNA Mexican Bar & Grill in Murrells Inlet is the one Mexican spot on our date-night list.

On a budget. Nearly every place here shows one dollar sign, the lowest price level on our map.

When are Mexican restaurants in Myrtle Beach busiest?

Friday and Saturday evenings are the busiest, along with Cinco de Mayo. Lunch is usually the easiest time to walk right in.

Hours can change in the off-season. Use the open-now filter on the map or call before you drive over.

Local tips

  • Go at lunch for the shortest wait.
  • Have a big group? Call ahead so the restaurant can push tables together.
  • Taquerias are a good takeout pick for the beach or the condo.
  • Several restaurants share the El Cerro name. Check the address on the listing so you drive to the right one.
  • Check hours before you go in winter. Some spots close earlier in the off-season.

Common questions

Where can I get good Mexican food in Myrtle Beach?

In Myrtle Beach, try Tu Taco or Taqueria Guanajuato for tacos, or Mexico Lindo, Margaritas Mexican Restaurant or El Cerro Mexican Bar & Grill for a sit-down meal.

Are there Mexican restaurants in North Myrtle Beach?

Yes. Craft Taco, Roca Roja Cantina, La Promesa Taqueria Y Pupuseria and El Cerro Mexican Bar & Grill are all in North Myrtle Beach, and Casa Villa is nearby in Little River.

Is there a Mexican restaurant for date night near Myrtle Beach?

LUNA Mexican Bar & Grill in Murrells Inlet is on our date-night list.
