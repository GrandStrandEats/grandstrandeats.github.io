New Year's Eve questions

Where can I have New Year's Eve dinner in Myrtle Beach?

Avista Resort in North Myrtle Beach has confirmed its 2026 New Year's Eve dinner package. In past years 21 Main Prime Steakhouse, House of Blues, Riptydz, Waterscapes at the Marina Inn, Hard Rock Cafe and the MarshWalk restaurants in Murrells Inlet have all hosted New Year's Eve dinners or parties.

How much is New Year's Eve dinner in Myrtle Beach?

Last year prices ran from a $20 cover at Hard Rock Cafe to $119–$129 for dinner and a show at House of Blues and $175 per person for the 4-course party at 21 Main Prime Steakhouse. Most 2026 prices are not posted yet.

Where are the New Year's Eve fireworks in Myrtle Beach?

Last year Barefoot Landing set off fireworks at 7 PM, Broadway at the Beach at 8 PM and the Myrtle Beach Boardwalk at 9 PM, with a midnight show in downtown Conway. Check times closer to the day.

Is there a family-friendly New Year's Eve in Myrtle Beach?

Yes. LuLu's in North Myrtle Beach has held a free Noon Year's Eve countdown for kids around 10 AM, and the early fireworks at Barefoot Landing and Broadway at the Beach work well for families.
