Where can I hear live music in Myrtle Beach tonight?

Head to an oceanfront beach bar in Myrtle Beach, the MarshWalk in Murrells Inlet, or North Myrtle Beach. Those three areas have the most live music spots on our map.

Oceanfront beach bars. Ocean Annie's, RipTydz Oceanfront Grille & Rooftop Bar, 8th Avenue Tiki Bar & Grill and Bummz Beach Cafe & Bar are all on the ocean in Myrtle Beach. Snooky's Oceanfront is the oceanfront pick in North Myrtle Beach.

Murrells Inlet and the MarshWalk. Dead Dog Saloon, Creek Ratz and Wahoo's Fish House are on the MarshWalk, where you can walk from one band to the next. Hot Fish Club, Outriggers Bar & Grill, On the Half Shell, O'Keefe's Irish Pub, Fire To Table and Suck Bang Blow are nearby in Murrells Inlet.

North Myrtle Beach. O D Lounge is a shag dancing bar, and OD Arcade & Lounge is another music spot. Big Chill Island House, LuLu's North Myrtle Beach, Dick's Last Resort and Molly Darcy's Irish Pub & Restaurant are all on the water.

Around Myrtle Beach. The Bowery is a country music bar. Dueling Pianos, Broadway Louie's, Finn McCool's, Jake's Dive Bar, 44 & King, Señor Frogs and Boathouse Waterway Bar & Grill are also marked for live music.

South end. Wild Horse Saloon and Fork'n Links are in Garden City, Neal & Pam's Bar and Grill is in Surfside Beach, and Pawleys Island Tavern is in Pawleys Island.

Which live music spot fits my night?

Pick a waterfront restaurant for dinner with music, a beach bar for an afternoon, and a pub or dance bar for a late night.

Dinner with music. Wahoo's Fish House and Hot Fish Club are also on our date-night list. Villa Romana is an Italian restaurant marked for music. LuLu's, Creek Ratz, Outriggers and Fire To Table are marked family-friendly.

An afternoon by the water. The oceanfront bars and the MarshWalk are good for a daytime drink with a view.

With a dog. Snooky's Oceanfront, Boathouse Waterway Bar & Grill and Neal & Pam's Bar and Grill are marked dog-friendly on our map. Call ahead to confirm.

When is live music season in Myrtle Beach?

Summer has the most live music, with outdoor stages busiest from Memorial Day through Labor Day. Many places keep music going on weekends the rest of the year.

Outdoor shows depend on the weather, and schedules change often. Check the bar's Facebook page or website for who is playing and what time.

Local tips

  • Check the venue's Facebook page the day you go. That's where most local bars post that week's bands.
  • On the MarshWalk you can park once and walk between several spots with music.
  • Go early for a table near the stage on summer weekends. Seats near the band fill first.
  • Outdoor music can be cancelled for rain or wind. Have an indoor backup.
  • Bringing kids? Go for dinner-hour music at a family-friendly restaurant, and ask about age rules for later at night.

Common questions

Where is the best place for live music in Myrtle Beach?

For music on the ocean, try Ocean Annie's, RipTydz or 8th Avenue Tiki Bar & Grill in Myrtle Beach. For several spots within a short walk, go to the Murrells Inlet MarshWalk, where Dead Dog Saloon, Creek Ratz and Wahoo's Fish House all have music.

Where can I go shag dancing in North Myrtle Beach?

O D Lounge in North Myrtle Beach is a shag dancing bar.

Are there family-friendly restaurants with live music near Myrtle Beach?

Yes. LuLu's North Myrtle Beach, Creek Ratz, Outriggers Bar & Grill and Fire To Table are marked both family-friendly and live music on our map. Go at dinner time and check the schedule first.
