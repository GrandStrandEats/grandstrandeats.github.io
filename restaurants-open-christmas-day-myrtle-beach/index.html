Christmas Day dining questions

Are restaurants open on Christmas Day in Myrtle Beach?

Yes. Several Myrtle Beach restaurants are open on Christmas Day 2026, including Black Drum Brewing at Kingston Resorts, Riptydz, Thoroughbreds, New York Prime and Hard Rock Cafe. Most locally owned spots close, so book early.

Is there a Christmas Day buffet in Myrtle Beach?

Black Drum Brewing at Kingston Resorts is serving a family-style Christmas feast from 11 AM to 6 PM for $54 per adult. Carolina Seafood & Steak has hosted a Christmas Day buffet in past years but has not posted 2026 plans yet.

Do I need a reservation for Christmas dinner?

Yes, for sit-down restaurants. Christmas Day tables fill up quickly in Myrtle Beach, especially evening steakhouse seatings.
