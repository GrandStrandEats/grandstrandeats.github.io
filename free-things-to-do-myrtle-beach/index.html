What can you do in Myrtle Beach for free?

Walk the Boardwalk, stroll Broadway at the Beach or Barefoot Landing, watch the sunset on the Murrells Inlet MarshWalk, and visit the free Horry County Museum. The beach itself is free too, with public access points all along the Strand.

Nature lovers can hike Lewis Ocean Bay Heritage Preserve, one of the few places where Venus flytraps grow wild, or walk the shaded Conway Riverwalk.

Are there free things to do with kids in Myrtle Beach?

Yes. The L.W. Paul Living History Farm in Conway is free and has farm animals and demonstrations. The Horry County Museum is free too.

Grand Park at Market Common has playgrounds and a lake, and Hurl Rock Park has the rocky tide pools kids love at low tide.

Is the Myrtle Beach Boardwalk free?

Yes. Walking the Boardwalk is free any time. You only pay for the rides, arcades, shops and food along the way.

Local tips

  • Parking near the Boardwalk and beach accesses is often paid in season. Check the signs.
  • The Horry County Museum is open weekdays, so plan a weekday visit.
  • Low tide is the best time for Hurl Rock Park's coquina rocks and tide pools.
  • Bring bug spray to Lewis Ocean Bay, especially in warm months.

Common questions

Is the beach free in Myrtle Beach?

Yes. The beach is public and free. Some beach access lots charge for parking in season.

What is free to do in Myrtle Beach at night?

Walk the Boardwalk, browse Broadway at the Beach, or stroll the MarshWalk in Murrells Inlet and listen to the live music.

Are there free museums near Myrtle Beach?

Yes. The Horry County Museum in Conway and its L.W. Paul Living History Farm are both free.
