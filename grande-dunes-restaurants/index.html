Where is Grande Dunes in Myrtle Beach?

Grande Dunes is a resort and residential area in the north end of Myrtle Beach, stretching from the ocean across Kings Highway to the Intracoastal Waterway. It includes the Grande Dunes Ocean Club on the beach and the Marina Inn at Grande Dunes on the waterway, plus golf and homes in between.

It's about 15 minutes north of Broadway at the Beach and about 15 minutes south of Barefoot Landing, depending on traffic.

What are the best restaurants near Grande Dunes?

For a nice dinner near Grande Dunes, Hook and Barrel is the closest sit-down seafood spot on our date-night list. For something casual, Liberty Tap Room is a brewpub with a big menu, and Blueberry's Grill is the breakfast and lunch pick.

Brunch. Heirloom Bistro does Southern cooking and brunch, and Mimosa Restaurant is a brunch spot a little farther south.

With kids. Pirate's Voyage Dinner & Show is just up Kings Highway. Ducati's Pizza Trattoria does pizza and Italian, and Cafe Gelato is close by for dessert.

Other cuisines nearby. Our map also lists Opa Opa Taverna (Greek), Peno Mediterranean Grill, Fortune Asian Bistro, Gios Italian Kitchen and Lombardo's Italian Restaurant within about a mile.

Is there anywhere to eat on the water near Grande Dunes?

The closest waterfront dining is on the Intracoastal side near the marina, or at the beach bars along the oceanfront. For a full list of local spots with a water view, see our waterfront restaurants guide.

Local tips

  • Kings Highway in north Myrtle Beach is lined with restaurants. Most of the spots below are a short drive, not a walk, from the resort.
  • Summer dinner waits are long at the popular seafood spots. Go before 5:30 PM or call ahead.
  • Staying a week? Liberty Tap Room and Blueberry's Grill are easy, casual picks for a night you don't want to plan.
  • Pirate's Voyage is a dinner show, so book tickets ahead instead of just showing up.

Common questions

Is it Grand Dunes or Grande Dunes?

The official name is Grande Dunes, with an e, but lots of people search for Grand Dunes. It's the same place: a resort area in north Myrtle Beach between the ocean and the Intracoastal Waterway.

What restaurants are near Grande Dunes in Myrtle Beach?

Local spots close by include Hook and Barrel for seafood, Liberty Tap Room for a casual brewpub dinner, Blueberry's Grill for breakfast and lunch, Heirloom Bistro for brunch, and Pirate's Voyage Dinner & Show for families.

Where can I get breakfast near Grande Dunes?

Blueberry's Grill serves breakfast and lunch, and Heirloom Bistro and Mimosa Restaurant do brunch. See our breakfast guide for more.
