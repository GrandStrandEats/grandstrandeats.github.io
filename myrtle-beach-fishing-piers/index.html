Do you need a fishing license to fish from a pier in Myrtle Beach?

Usually not. South Carolina lets licensed piers cover their anglers, so you pay the pier's fishing fee instead of buying your own saltwater license. Ask at the pier house to be sure.

Can you walk on Myrtle Beach piers without fishing?

Yes. Most piers let you walk out for a small fee or for free. They are great for sunrise, dolphin spotting and photos. Prices vary by pier and season.

Local tips

  • Most piers rent rods and sell bait, so you don't need to bring gear.
  • Fall is a favorite fishing season here, with cooler weather and fewer crowds.
  • Piers close during storms and lightning.

Common questions

What is the longest pier in Myrtle Beach?

Apache Pier, at Apache Family Campground, bills itself as the longest wooden pier on the East Coast.

Which Myrtle Beach pier is best for families?

The pier at Myrtle Beach State Park and the Surfside Beach Pier are quieter and easy with kids.
