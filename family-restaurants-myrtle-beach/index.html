What are the best kid-friendly restaurants in Myrtle Beach?

For breakfast with kids, go to a pancake house. For dinner, a seafood buffet, pizza place or barbecue joint keeps everyone happy, and a dinner show makes it a night out.

Pancake houses. Donald's Pancake House and Pan American Pancake & Omelette House in Myrtle Beach, Golden Egg Pancake House, Omega Pancake House and Johnny D's Waffles in Surfside Beach, and Hot Stacks Pancake House in North Myrtle Beach.

Seafood buffets. Captain George's Seafood Restaurant and The Original Benjamin's Calabash Buffet in Myrtle Beach, The Giant Crab in North Myrtle Beach, and Crabby Mike's Calabash Seafood in Surfside Beach. Buffets let picky eaters fill up on whatever they like.

Barbecue. Little Pigs BBQ, Fullbelly BBQ and Simply Southern Smokehouse in Myrtle Beach, and Big D's BBQ Trough in North Myrtle Beach.

Are there dinner shows for kids in Myrtle Beach?

Yes. Pirate's Voyage Dinner & Show and Medieval Times are both dinner shows in Myrtle Beach where you eat while you watch. They're among the most popular family nights out on the Grand Strand. Book tickets ahead, especially in summer.

Where can I eat with kids on the water?

LuLu's and Flying Fish at Barefoot Landing, Creek Ratz and Drunken Jack's on the Murrells Inlet MarshWalk, and the Pier Snack Bar in Garden City are all marked family-friendly on our map. Kids can watch boats or the waves while you wait.

Where can I get ice cream with kids?

Painter's Homemade Ice Cream has spots in Surfside Beach and North Myrtle Beach, and Mad Myrtle's Ice Creamery is in Myrtle Beach. Ice cream after dinner is a beach tradition, so expect a line on summer nights.

Local tips

  • Eat dinner early, around 4:30 or 5 PM. You'll beat the summer waits, and tired kids do better.
  • Buffets and pizza are the easiest way to feed a big group without a long wait for every plate.
  • Pancake houses are busiest on weekend mornings. Go before 8 AM or after 10.
  • Many places have high chairs and kids' menus, but call ahead if you need a big table.
  • Bring a small bag of crayons or a card game for the wait on busy nights.

Common questions

What are good family restaurants in Myrtle Beach?

Popular local picks include pancake houses like Donald's Pancake House, seafood buffets like Captain George's, barbecue at Little Pigs BBQ or Simply Southern Smokehouse, and dinner shows like Pirate's Voyage and Medieval Times.

Where can I take kids to breakfast in Myrtle Beach?

Donald's Pancake House and Pan American Pancake & Omelette House in Myrtle Beach, Golden Egg Pancake House and Johnny D's Waffles in Surfside Beach, or Hot Stacks Pancake House in North Myrtle Beach.

Are there seafood buffets in Myrtle Beach?

Yes. Captain George's Seafood Restaurant, The Original Benjamin's Calabash Buffet, The Giant Crab in North Myrtle Beach and Crabby Mike's in Surfside Beach are all on our map.
