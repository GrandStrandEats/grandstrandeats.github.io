What is there to do in Myrtle Beach besides the beach?

Plenty: state parks, fishing piers, an oceanfront boardwalk, an aquarium, dinner shows, live music theaters, gardens and history museums. Most of the big attractions sit along Highway 17 between North Myrtle Beach and Murrells Inlet.

For a first visit, walk the Boardwalk and ride the SkyWheel, spend a rainy afternoon at Ripley's Aquarium, catch a show at the Carolina Opry or Alabama Theatre, and drive south to Brookgreen Gardens and Huntington Beach State Park.

What is Myrtle Beach known for?

Myrtle Beach is known for 60 miles of beach, golf, seafood, live shows and family attractions. The Grand Strand runs from Little River near the North Carolina line down to Pawleys Island.

North Myrtle Beach is the home of the shag, South Carolina's state dance, and Murrells Inlet calls itself the seafood capital of South Carolina.

What can you do in Myrtle Beach in the off-season?

Shows, museums, the aquarium, state parks and the piers stay open in the cooler months. Some outdoor attractions, like the water park and amusement park, close for the winter.

Christmas shows run from early November at the Carolina Opry, Alabama Theatre, Pirates Voyage and Legends in Concert. Fall and winter are also a great time for the state parks, with fewer bugs and smaller crowds.

Local tips

  • Hours change a lot by season here. Check the attraction's website the day you go.
  • Broadway at the Beach puts Ripley's Aquarium, WonderWorks and the Hollywood Wax Museum within walking distance of each other.
  • Huntington Beach State Park, Atalaya and Brookgreen Gardens sit across the highway from each other. Make it one day trip.
  • Book show tickets ahead in summer and during the holiday season.
  • Summer Saturdays are check-in day for a lot of rentals, so Highway 17 and 501 get heavy traffic.

Common questions

What is the most popular attraction in Myrtle Beach?

The beach and the Boardwalk draw the most people. For a ticketed attraction, Ripley's Aquarium at Broadway at the Beach is one of the most popular, especially on rainy days.

Is Myrtle Beach good for families?

Yes. Between the aquarium, mini golf, go-karts, state parks, piers and dinner shows, there is plenty for kids of every age.

What is there to do in Myrtle Beach at night?

Walk the Boardwalk and ride the SkyWheel, see a show at the Carolina Opry, Alabama Theatre or Pirates Voyage, or head to the Murrells Inlet MarshWalk for live music on the water.
