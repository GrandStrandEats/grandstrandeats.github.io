What's the best romantic restaurant in Myrtle Beach for my kind of night?

Pick Sea Captain's House or Gulfstream Cafe for a sunset water view, Aspen Grille or Thoroughbreds for a dressy dinner, and Toscana or Villa Romana for Italian.

A water view at sunset. Sea Captain's House sits right on the ocean in Myrtle Beach. Gulfstream Cafe in Garden City looks out over the inlet. Wicked Tuna, Wahoo's Fish House and Hot Fish Club are all in Murrells Inlet, and Clark's Seafood and Chophouse is on the water in Little River. Joe's Bar & Grill in North Myrtle Beach is another water-view pick.

A dressy, special-occasion dinner. Aspen Grille, Thoroughbreds, SeaBlue Restaurant and Wine Bar and 21 Main Prime Steakhouse are the most upscale spots on this list, with prices to match. Flamingo Grill and Hook and Barrel are good for steak and seafood in a nice room.

Italian. Toscana Italian Kitchen and Bar, Villa Romana, Travinia Italian Kitchen, Villa Tuscanna, Benny's Trattoria and Angelo's Steak & Pasta all made our date-night list.

Something a little different. Rioz Brazilian Steakhouse carves meat at your table. Co Sushi and Art Burger Sushi Bar are fun for a more relaxed date, Bistro90 is another good North Myrtle Beach choice, and Heirloom Bistro works well for a brunch date.

How do I plan a special date night in Myrtle Beach?

Reserve early, especially for summer weekends and around Valentine's Day, and tell the restaurant if it's a birthday, anniversary or proposal. If a place doesn't take reservations, go early.

Many restaurants will try to give you a better table for a birthday, anniversary or proposal.

Dress on the Grand Strand is relaxed. Even at the nicer spots, resort casual is fine: a collared shirt or a sundress. Save the flip-flops for the beach bars.

Plan a walk after dinner. The Murrells Inlet MarshWalk, the Myrtle Beach Boardwalk, Barefoot Landing and the Market Common all make an easy, pretty stroll.

Local tips

  • Book ahead for Friday and Saturday nights from Memorial Day through Labor Day. Off-season weeknights are much easier.
  • Ask for a window or patio table when you book if you want a water view. Those go first.
  • Check sunset time before you go. Inlet and marsh views are at their best in the half hour before dark.
  • Several of these are on the Murrells Inlet MarshWalk, where you can walk the boardwalk after dinner.
  • Mention a birthday or anniversary when you reserve. It can make a difference in where you're seated.

Common questions

What's the most romantic restaurant in Myrtle Beach?

It depends on the night you want. For a sunset view, try Sea Captain's House on the ocean or Gulfstream Cafe on the inlet. For a dressy dinner, Aspen Grille or Thoroughbreds. For Italian, Toscana or Villa Romana.

Do I need a reservation for date night in Myrtle Beach?

For Friday and Saturday nights in summer, yes, if the restaurant takes them. Off-season weeknights are much easier, and you can often walk in.

What should I wear to a nice dinner in Myrtle Beach?

Resort casual works almost everywhere: nice shorts or slacks with a collared shirt, or a sundress. A few upscale spots lean dressier, so call ahead if you're unsure.
