Garden City Beach restaurants

Garden City Beach sits between Surfside Beach and Murrells Inlet, with the Garden City Pier at its center and the inlet on its back side. It's casual and laid back, with plenty of spots to grab a meal in flip-flops. Here is every local restaurant in Garden City on our map.
