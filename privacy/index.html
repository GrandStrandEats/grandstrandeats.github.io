Privacy policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

Grand Strand Eats (grandstrandeats.com) does not ask you to sign up, does not have forms, and does not collect your name or email address.

Saved spots and "Near me"

Spots you save are kept in your own browser's local storage. They stay on your device and are never sent to us. If you tap "Near me," your browser asks for permission to share your location. Your location is used only inside your browser to sort the list by distance. It is not sent to us or stored.

Offline use

The site saves a copy of its own files in your browser so it loads quickly and works with a weak signal at the beach. You can clear this at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Services that load with the site

Like any website, these services receive your IP address and basic browser information when your browser loads files from them:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the site and may keep server logs for security.
  • Google Fonts, for the site's font.
  • OpenFreeMap, for the map tiles, and cdnjs and unpkg, for the map code.

Affiliate links

Some links go to GetYourGuide and to hotel searches through Stay22 and Booking.com. When you click one of these, those companies may set cookies so they can tell that your booking came from us. Their own privacy policies apply on their sites.

Advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads to you based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of some other vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.

Analytics

We do not use analytics or tracking scripts on this site right now. If that changes, we will update this page.

Children

This site is meant for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too. Questions? See the contact page.
