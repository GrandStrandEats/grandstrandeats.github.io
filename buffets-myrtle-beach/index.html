Where can I get all-you-can-eat crab legs in Myrtle Beach?

Captain George's, Captain Benjamin's, The Original Benjamin's and Crabby Mike's all list crab legs on their seafood buffets. Captain George's and Captain Benjamin's are in Myrtle Beach, The Original Benjamin's is on Restaurant Row, and Crabby Mike's is in Surfside Beach.

The Giant Crab in North Myrtle Beach is another long-running Calabash-style seafood buffet near Barefoot Landing.

How much is a seafood buffet in Myrtle Beach?

Expect about $45 to $55 per adult for a seafood buffet with crab legs. Captain George's lists its all-you-can-eat seafood buffet at $51.99, with a Sunday adult buffet for $46.99 from noon to 4 PM. Captain Benjamin's early bird price is $44.99 if you're seated before 5 PM, $10 off the regular price.

Kids' prices are lower at most buffets, and some run weekday specials. Check each restaurant's website or Facebook page before you go.

What is a Calabash seafood buffet?

Calabash style means seafood that's lightly breaded and fried, named after the fishing town of Calabash, North Carolina, just north of the Grand Strand. A Calabash buffet adds crab legs, steamed shrimp, oysters, fried fish, hush puppies and Southern sides, usually with a dessert bar.

Are there buffets in Myrtle Beach besides seafood?

Yes. Sakura Hibachi Buffet in North Myrtle Beach and Taste of Asia Buffet in Myrtle Beach serve Asian buffets, and Judy Boone's in Murrells Inlet and Coppers in Conway serve Southern-style food. These cost less than the seafood buffets and work well for a quick family meal.

Local tips

  • Go early. Several seafood buffets charge less if you're seated before 5 PM, and lines get long at dinner in summer.
  • Crab legs come out in batches. Ask staff when the next tray is coming instead of taking cold ones.
  • Bring wipes. Cracking crab legs gets messy fast.
  • Most seafood buffets open in the afternoon, not for lunch. Check hours before you drive over.
  • Holiday buffets (Thanksgiving, Mother's Day, Easter) often cost more and need reservations.
