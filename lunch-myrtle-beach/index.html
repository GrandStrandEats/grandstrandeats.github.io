Where can I get a quick local lunch in Myrtle Beach?

For a quick, cheap lunch in Myrtle Beach, try barbecue at Little Pigs BBQ, Fullbelly BBQ or Simply Southern Smokehouse, tacos at Tu Taco, or soul food at Big Mike's Soul Food. All of them show one dollar sign on our map.

Burgers. Grumpy Monk does burgers and craft beer, Thorny's Steakhouse & Saloon has burgers too, and Art Burger Sushi Bar downtown does both burgers and sushi.

Lunch with a view. RipTydz Oceanfront Grille & Rooftop Bar serves burgers on a rooftop over the ocean.

Breakfast that runs into lunch. Blueberry's Grill serves breakfast and lunch, so it's a good pick if your group can't agree on which meal it is.

Where can I get lunch in North Myrtle Beach?

In North Myrtle Beach, Kitchen Table serves breakfast and lunch, Taco Mundo does tacos, and Big D's BBQ Trough does barbecue. Souffle's is a brunch café if you want something a little nicer.

Barefoot Landing and Main Street both have more casual lunch spots within walking distance of each other. See our Barefoot Landing guide and North Myrtle Beach guide.

Where can I get lunch in Surfside Beach, Garden City or Murrells Inlet?

Surfside Beach has Hamburger Joe's, River City Cafe, Surf Diner and Fat Jack's Wings & Things. Garden City has the Pier Snack Bar, right on the pier. Murrells Inlet has Sam's Corner for hot dogs, River City Cafe and The Grumpy Mustache.

The Carolina Food Truck Park in Murrells Inlet has a rotating lineup of food trucks, so it's a good stop when everyone wants something different.

Down in Pawleys Island, Village Deli & Creamery does sandwiches and ice cream.

Where can I get lunch in the Market Common or Conway?

At the Market Common, Bowled does poke and bowls, and Tidal Creek Brewhouse has a café menu with its beer.

In Conway, Cheesesteak U does sandwiches, Kosta Bowls does bowls, Crafty Rooster does burgers, Chantis does pizza and sandwiches, and CW's Wings and Ribs and Toyama Hibachi and Wings both do wings. See our Conway guide for more.

When is the best time to get lunch at the beach?

Eat before noon or after 1:30 PM in summer to skip the lunch rush. On rainy days every lunch spot near the beach fills up at once, so go early or call ahead for takeout.

Lunch is also the cheapest way to try a popular dinner spot. Many sit-down restaurants have lighter lunch menus and shorter waits in the middle of the day.

Local tips

  • Call your order in and pick it up on the way back to the beach. It beats waiting in line in a wet swimsuit.
  • Barbecue and taco shops are some of the cheapest ways to feed a group.
  • Want a waterfront meal without the sunset crowd? Go to a waterfront spot for lunch instead of dinner.
  • Some lunch spots close mid-afternoon or take a day off in the off-season. Check today's hours on the map.
  • Rainy day? Expect a crowd everywhere around noon. Go at 11 or after 1:30.
