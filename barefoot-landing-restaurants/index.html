What are the best restaurants at Barefoot Landing?

For dinner on the water at Barefoot Landing, try LuLu's, Big Chill Island House, Flying Fish or Dick's Last Resort. All four are on the Intracoastal Waterway side.

LuLu's is a Gulf Coast-style seafood spot with live music, and it's marked family-friendly on our map.

Big Chill Island House is a big waterfront bar and grill with live music, good for a drink at sunset.

Flying Fish is a seafood restaurant on the water, and it's marked family-friendly too.

Dick's Last Resort is famous for its rude-on-purpose servers. It's a fun night out, but not for everyone.

Where can I get a cheap meal or a treat near Barefoot Landing?

For something quick and cheap near Barefoot Landing, try Taco Mundo for tacos, Ultimate California Pizza, or River City Cafe for burgers.

For breakfast, Blueberry's Grill and Kitchen Table are both close by.

For dessert, our map lists The Crafty Cow and Sweet Molly's Creamery for ice cream.

Is Barefoot Landing worth visiting for dinner?

Yes. It's one of the easiest places on the Grand Strand to eat on the water and then walk around afterward. The shops and boardwalks are free to stroll, and the Alabama Theatre is right there if you want to make a night of it.

Barefoot Landing sits on Highway 17 in North Myrtle Beach, about 25 to 30 minutes north of Broadway at the Beach. For more spots in the area, see our North Myrtle Beach guide.

Local tips

  • Ask for a table by the water when you put your name in. Those go first at sunset.
  • Summer weekend waits can be long. Eat before 5:30 PM or after 8 PM.
  • Seeing a show at the Alabama Theatre? Eat early so you're not rushing.
  • Bring bug spray for waterfront tables around dusk.
