What restaurants are on Main Street in North Myrtle Beach?

Main Street has pizza, pancakes, seafood, bagels, Irish and Cuban spots, plus ice cream and milkshake shops, all within a short walk. Pizza is easy to find here: Georgio's Famous Pizza, The Main Slice and Daniello's Pizzeria & Pub are all on our map. Castaway Keys on Main does seafood and a raw bar, and Golden Griddle Pancake House and The Bagel Factory cover breakfast.

For dessert, Main Sweets, Melt and The Crazy Mason Milkshake Bar are all close by.

Where can I go shag dancing on Main Street?

The O.D. Lounge and OD Arcade & Lounge near the ocean end of Main Street are part of the shag dancing scene that North Myrtle Beach's Ocean Drive section is famous for. The shag is South Carolina's state dance, and the Ocean Drive area is where it grew up. Check each bar's own page for music nights before you go.

Is Main Street North Myrtle Beach walkable?

Yes. The ocean end of Main Street is a compact, walkable strip of restaurants, bars and shops a block from the beach. Park once and walk between dinner, ice cream and the beach. The spots near Highway 17 are a short drive up the road.

Local tips

  • Park once near the ocean end of Main Street and walk. Most spots are a few minutes apart.
  • Pizza by the slice is the quick, cheap option on Main Street.
  • Main Street gets busy on summer evenings and during shag dance events, so go early for dinner.
  • Check hours before you go. Some spots cut back their hours in the off-season.
