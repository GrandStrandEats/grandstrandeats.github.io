Where should I eat at Broadway at the Beach?

For a sit-down family meal at Broadway at the Beach, try Paula Deen's Family Kitchen for Southern food or Yamato for hibachi. For a party-style night out, head to Celebrity Square.

Southern and family style. Paula Deen's Family Kitchen serves Southern dishes family style, which works well for groups.

Hibachi. Yamato cooks at the table, which kids tend to love.

Nightlife. Celebrity Square is Broadway's bar section. Señor Frog's is a Mexican restaurant and party bar, Wet Willie's does frozen daiquiris, and Dueling Pianos and Broadway Louie's have live music.

Snacks and treats. Our map lists coffee, popcorn and ice cream shops in and around Broadway, including Frapp'a Joe, Social Coffee, Sweet South Popcorn Company and The Pineapple Hut.

Is it worth eating at Broadway at the Beach?

Yes, if you're already there for the shops or attractions. Expect tourist prices and crowds at peak times. Most spots here show two dollar signs on our map.

If you want local seafood instead of a theme-park feel, drive 20 to 30 minutes south to the Murrells Inlet MarshWalk, or see our seafood guide.

When is Broadway at the Beach busiest?

Summer evenings and rainy days are the busiest times, so eat before 5:30 PM or after 8 PM if you can. Weekends from late May through Labor Day are the most crowded.

In the off-season some spots shorten their hours or close a day or two a week. Check the map for today's hours before you go.

Local tips

  • Put your name in at a restaurant first, then walk the shops while you wait.
  • Rainy beach days send everyone to Broadway at once. Go early for lunch.
  • Celebrity Square gets loud late at night. For a quieter dinner with kids, eat earlier.
  • Bigger groups do better with family-style or hibachi spots, where everyone eats at once.

Common questions

What restaurants are at Broadway at the Beach?

Local spots on our map at or near Broadway include Paula Deen's Family Kitchen, Yamato hibachi, Señor Frog's, Wet Willie's, Dueling Pianos and Broadway Louie's, plus coffee, popcorn and ice cream shops.

What is Celebrity Square at Broadway at the Beach?

It's the nightlife section of Broadway at the Beach, with bars, live music and party-style restaurants. It's busiest late in the evening.

Where is a good family restaurant at Broadway at the Beach?

Paula Deen's Family Kitchen serves Southern food family style, and Yamato does hibachi cooked at the table. Both are marked family-friendly on our map.
