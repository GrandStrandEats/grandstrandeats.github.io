Conway, SC restaurants

Conway is the Horry County seat, about 20 minutes inland from Myrtle Beach on the Waccamaw River. Its historic downtown and Riverwalk make a nice break from the beach crowds, and it's home to Coastal Carolina University. Here is every local restaurant in Conway on our map.

Local tips

  • Downtown Conway is small and walkable. Park once and walk to dinner and the Riverwalk.
  • Coastal Carolina home games and graduation weekends get busy, so plan ahead on those dates.
